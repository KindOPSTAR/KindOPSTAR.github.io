He "Albert" Zhang

He Zhang, or Albert is a HCI researcher. He is a third-year PhD student in Informatics at the College of Information Science and Technology, Penn State University, advised by Dr. John M. Carroll. He is a student member of the Center for Socially Responsible Artificial Intelligence (CSRAI), and the winner of IST Ph.D. Student Award for Research Excellence. He was a full-time research assistant (2021-2022) in an interdisciplinary human-computer interaction lab (The Future Lab) at Tsinghua University. He owned Bachelor of Science degree (2019) with double-major in Computer Science & Data Science, issued by Massey University, New Zealand, and Master of Science degree (2021) with majors in Computer and Mathematical Sciences, issued by Auckland University of Technology.
